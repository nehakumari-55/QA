A QA journey built on curiosity.

Testing became more than finding defects: it became a way of understanding products, people, risks and the systems connecting them.

Where it started

With a Computer Science & Engineering background, I began my professional journey close to the software development lifecycle. Early QA work taught me the fundamentals: understand the requirement, question the assumption, reproduce the problem, and make the result visible.

How it evolved

Over time, my role moved from execution toward engineering: automation frameworks, API and integration testing, CI/CD, regression strategy, structured test management and closer collaboration with product and development teams. Today I combine that foundation with SAP-focused testing and AI-assisted QA practices.
